Good to know

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a real baker will reply.

Can every cake be made eggless?

Yes. Every cake and cupcake comes in an eggless version for a small extra charge. Our cookies are always eggless. Macarons need egg whites, so they are the only exception.

How late can I order for same-day delivery?

Order before 4 PM and choose any slot that starts at least 3 hours later. The wedding cake needs 3 days' notice.

How does midnight delivery work?

Pick the midnight slot when you check out. We deliver between 11:30 PM and 12 AM so the surprise lands right on time.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Bengaluru. Type your pincode in your box to check.

How do I pay?

Once you send your order on WhatsApp, we confirm it and share a UPI link. Cash on delivery is available for orders under ₹2,000.
