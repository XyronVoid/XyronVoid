06FAQ

How does the 100-night sofa trial work?

Live with your sofa for up to 100 nights. If you don't love it, we'll pick it up and refund you in full, minus delivery.

What if I live upstairs without a lift?

No problem. Pick your floor in the cart and we show the stairs charge upfront: a small fee per floor for each large item.

Is assembly included?

Assembly is free on orders above ₹25,000. Below that, add it per item in your cart. Our team assembles within 48 hours of delivery.

How do EMIs work?

Pay in 3, 6, 9 or 12 monthly instalments at 0% interest on most credit cards and UPI pay-later apps. Choose a tenure on any product.
