N° 05 — Find your ring size

No ring sizer? No problem.

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper or thread around the base of your finger.
  2. Mark where it meets, then lay it flat and measure the length in millimetres.
  3. Enter it here. We'll show your Indian ring size.

N° 07 — Care

Made to last a lifetime

Can I shower and swim in it?

Yes. Vermeil and solid gold are both fine in water. Rinse off chlorine and salt water and pat dry to keep the shine.

What if the gold wears off?

Send your vermeil piece back any time and we'll replate it for free, for life. You only pay shipping.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

All our pieces are nickel-free and lead-free. The base is hallmarked 925 sterling silver.

How does engraving work?

Add up to 10 characters on bracelets and necklaces. Engraved pieces ship in 3–4 days and can't be returned.

What's the return policy?

30 days, no questions asked, for unworn pieces in their original box. Earrings can't be returned for hygiene reasons.
