Good questions

What does the warranty cover?

If the shutter, meter or film advance stops working within 6 months, we repair it free or refund you. You can extend it to 18 months at checkout.

Where do I buy film?

Every camera comes with one free roll. Add a 3-roll starter pack in your bag, or buy from any photo store. 35mm and 120 film are easy to find.

Can I see the actual camera?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send close-up photos or a video of the exact camera before you pay.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, insured and packed in foam. Most orders arrive in 3–5 days.
