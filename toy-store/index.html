Questions? Answers!

Are the toys safe for babies?

Yes, if you pick embroidered eyes. They have no hard parts and are tested to the EN71 toy safety standard. Plastic safety eyes are for ages 3 and up.

Can I wash them?

Hand wash in cold water with a mild soap, or use a gentle machine cycle inside a pillow cover. Dry flat in the shade.

How long does delivery take?

Mini and Classic toys are ready to ship in 1–2 days. Huggable toys are made to order and ship in about 7 days.

What's on the adoption certificate?

Your toy's name, the adopter's name, the adoption date and the maker's signature, printed on thick card and tucked into the box.
